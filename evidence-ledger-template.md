# Evidence Ledger Template

A delivery claim should be reproducible by someone who was not present during the work. Use this ledger to connect each claim to a concrete observation.

The ticket history in [Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) can store the final narrative; this compact ledger keeps the underlying evidence consistent.

## Evidence quality rules

Good evidence is:

- **Direct:** it comes from the resulting page, artifact, log, or authoritative system.
- **Current:** it was checked after the final action.
- **Specific:** it proves the exact claim, not merely that a workflow was attempted.
- **Accessible:** the reviewer can open it with the expected permissions.
- **Stable:** it is unlikely to disappear when a draft session ends.

Do not use these as completion evidence:

- A filled form before submission.
- A success toast when the public result is missing.
- A pending moderation page.
- A search snippet without opening the result.
- A screenshot that hides the relevant URL or state.
