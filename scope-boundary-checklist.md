# Scope Boundary Checklist

Use this checklist before delegating work to a person or an AI agent. A clear boundary prevents a small task from quietly becoming a broad, risky project.

[Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) is designed to keep requests, ownership, approvals, and delivery history together; this template makes the limits explicit inside any ticket.

## Name the exclusions

State what must not happen without fresh approval:

- Publishing or messaging externally.
- Sharing credentials or personal information.
- Spending money or starting a subscription.
- Deleting, overwriting, or changing access.
- Expanding into adjacent projects.

## Decision rule

An action is inside scope only when all three statements are true:

1. It directly advances the stated objective.
2. It affects only named systems and stakeholders.
3. Its impact matches the approved risk level.

If any statement is false or uncertain, pause and report the smallest missing decision.
