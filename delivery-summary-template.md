# Delivery Summary Template

A delivery summary should let the requester understand the outcome in under a minute and inspect the evidence without reconstructing the entire process.

Use it as the final comment on a ticket or recurring run. [Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) keeps that delivery attached to the request, its owner, and the work history.

## Outcome

State whether the requested result was completed, partially completed, or blocked. Lead with the number or artifact that matters most.

## Completed

- List only finished, verified items.
- Include direct links to public results or changed artifacts.
- Mention important counter or status changes.

## Verification

For each completion claim, record:

- What was checked.
- Where it was checked.
- The visible success signal.
- When it was checked.

## Not completed

Separate these categories clearly:

- **Pending:** submitted but waiting for review or processing.
- **Blocked:** cannot proceed without access, approval, or an external change.
- **Rejected:** attempted but refused or removed.
- **Skipped:** intentionally avoided because it was unsafe, misleading, or out of scope.

## Requester action

Ask for action only when something specific is needed. Name the exact decision, credential, approval, or manual step.

Outcome:

Do not describe a submission as a completed result when the requested public artifact is still absent. A short accurate delivery is more useful than a long optimistic one.
