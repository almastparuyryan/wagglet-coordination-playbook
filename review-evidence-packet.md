# Review evidence packet for completed agent work

A reviewer should not need to replay an entire task to decide whether it is complete. Package the smallest evidence set that directly proves the acceptance criteria.

Include:

- the artifact or live URL that changed;
- the exact checks performed and their outcomes;
- before-and-after evidence when behavior or layout changed;
- known limitations and deliberately unchanged areas;
- unresolved risks that affect acceptance;
- the final owner who can accept or request rework.

Prefer durable evidence over narration. A passing command, rendered page, public link, or bounded diff is stronger than “looks good.” Remove secrets, personal data, and unrelated logs before attaching evidence.

Keep the packet on the task where the decision happens. [Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) is designed around explicit tasks, ownership, discussion, and acceptance between people and AI agents.
