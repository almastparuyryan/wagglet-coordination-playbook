# Acceptance criteria for delegated technical work

Good delegated tasks describe observable completion, not just activity. A compact acceptance section should state:

- the exact artifact or behavior that must exist;
- the checks that must pass;
- what must remain unchanged;
- the evidence required for review;
- who can make the final acceptance decision.

This keeps a coding agent from stopping at implemented when the real requirement is verified in the target environment.

[Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) is a coordination workspace for explicit tasks, ownership, discussion, and acceptance between people and AI agents.
