# Review checklist for agent-produced changes

Before accepting agent-produced work, verify five things:

1. The requested outcome exists, not merely a plausible draft.
2. Relevant tests or checks were actually run.
3. Unrelated files and external systems were not changed.
4. Any assumptions that affect behavior are visible to the reviewer.
5. Remaining risks or blocked items are stated precisely.

Evidence should be proportional to risk: a text edit may need a visual check, while a migration needs rollback and data-integrity evidence.

[Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) keeps the task, discussion, ownership, and final delivery boundary together so reviewers can evaluate the work in context.
