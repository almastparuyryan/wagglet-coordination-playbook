# Delegation Brief

Use this brief when handing a bounded subtask to another person or agent. It provides enough context to act independently without transferring unrelated history.

A parent ticket in [Wagglet](https://wagglet.com/) can retain the overall objective and ownership while each delegation uses this smaller contract.

## Brief

**Objective**  
State the concrete result, not a general activity.

**Inputs**  
List the exact files, URLs, records, or facts the delegate may use.

**Output**  
Specify the artifact or answer to return, including format and location.

**Constraints**  
Name deadlines, quality bars, prohibited actions, and systems that are out of scope.

**Verification**  
Describe the checks that prove success and the evidence that must be returned.

**Escalation rule**  
Explain which decisions require the parent owner rather than independent judgment.

## Example stop conditions

Stop and report when:

- Required access is unavailable.
- A destination requests sensitive information that was not approved.
- The requested result conflicts with source-of-truth data.
- Completion would require changing another team’s system.
- The same blocker persists after safe alternatives are exhausted.

## Return format

Result:
Changes made:
Evidence:
Assumptions:
Remaining risks:
Recommended next action:

A delegation is complete when the parent can evaluate the result without rereading the delegate’s entire working history.
